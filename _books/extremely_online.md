Curious how we got to today's sphere of online content creators? What did it take for online video platforms to achieve mass adoption and drive social change along the way? [Extremely Online](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/460663/extremely-online-by-lorenz-taylor/9780753560792) documents the history of this technology's development, outlining the predecessors of YouTube and TikTok along the trajectory from nascent digital communication to ubiquitous social dialogue. Given how much culture and history is now being shaped by these platforms, it's worth knowing how they came to prominence.
