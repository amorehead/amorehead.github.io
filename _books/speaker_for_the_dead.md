The sequel to Ender's Game, [Speaker for the Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_for_the_Dead), does not disappoint. In fact, its philosophical undertones came across rather naturally as the story wrestles with describing its protagonist as both savior of humanity and a genocidal mastermind (n.b., you were right, Kyle!). How would you view yourself if you were tricked into committing an alien genocide but later lauded by your species as a hero? Speaker for the Dead explores these themes with delicate precision, building on the worlds and characters of its well-known prequel.
