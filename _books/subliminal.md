How can old memories and nearly imperceptible phenomena sway what decisions we make? Answer: the unconscious says so. [Subliminal](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13058637-subliminal) captures the feeling of wonder that arises upon discovering how peculiar the human mind's inner workings truly are. Notably, the book is comprised of many first and third-hand accounts of contexts where one's mental intuitions have been automatically bypassed in favor of deep-seated cognitive templates our minds have developed over the years. As such, this work has implications for several aspects of our lives, including our relationships, vocations, and community structures.
