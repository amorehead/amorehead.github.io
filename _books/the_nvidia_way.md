What is there to say about Nvidia's history other than "woh"! From early graphics chip manufacturing for accelerating computing workflows (e.g., video games, simulations) to deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and now digital biology - the breadth and depth of applications of graphics processing hardware is hard to understate. Growing up, I wouldn't have predicted that the company that produced the graphics cards I liked to purchase for playing Steam PC games would become a pivotal element of the ongoing race to artificial general intelligence (AGI). Nonetheless, here we are, and to understand a bit of where the future may (further) lead, [The Nvidia Way](https://www.taekim.com/) offers some insights from Nvidia's past.
