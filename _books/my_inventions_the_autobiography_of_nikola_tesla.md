Tesla, oh Tesla...Nikola Tesla, that is. What an eccentric fellow, who went on to develop transformative technologies, such as the Tesla coil and the transformer (n.b., not the AI one). His [autobiography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Inventions:_The_Autobiography_of_Nikola_Tesla) poignantly presents Tesla's inner monologue as he grows and matures as both a human being and an inventor. There are many lessons we can learn from a life like Tesla's, such as the time and attention often required to become true creatives within a given industry. One part intuition, many parts perspiration.
