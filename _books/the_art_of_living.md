Ironically, even though Thich Nhat Hanh's [The Art of Living](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/29779239-the-art-of-living) and Michael Singer's [The Untethered Soul](https://untetheredsoul.com/untethered-soul) are topically related, Hanh's writing was much more evocative and illustrative to me. Hanh's use of analogy and storytelling, I believe, made the difference here. Instead of offering direct advice on how to improve your life, The Art of Living paints a picture of a life well lived, from which we can work backwards.
